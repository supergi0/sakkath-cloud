# Match Reporting

Both team admins report their own match independently. Each game has two reporters from opposite teams entering stats simultaneously.

## Before the game

- Reporting is locked until the round officially opens. You can view upcoming games but they won't be reportable yet.
- Once reporting is enabled, you can begin from the **My Team** section.
- During the toss, point out to the other team who will be keeping stats for most of the game.

## During the game

### Scoring events

Only register events for your own team. A simple check: if you don't recognise the name you're about to mark an event for, don't.

- **D (Defense)**: Mark when a defender makes contact with the disc during a turnover.
- **Turn on thrower**: Mark when the thrower loses the disc without the catcher making contact.
- **Turn on catcher**: Mark when the catcher had contact with the disc but the offense turns it over.
- Track every single point events, do not skip any.
- Events can be undone if needed, and all records are stored for dispute resolution.

### If things go wrong

- Network or battery issues: get help from the other team to keep stats going.
- Any ambiguity on what to mark: talk it out with the other team before marking.
- Any disputes: bring it to the tournament director, who will have the final say.

## After the game

Once the match and spirit circle completes, enter the following steps in the system:

- Confirm the final score and end the game.
- Submit spirit scores.
- Submit MVP and MSP selections.
