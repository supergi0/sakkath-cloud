# Tournament Rules

## Game Time

- All Swiss games are 65 minutes and to 13 points.
- Sunday playoff and placement games are 75 minutes and to 15 points.
- It is preferred to use the timer in website or self time games for accuracy.

- If a point ends after alloted time and teams are,
    - `not tied`: Game is hard stopped.
    - `tied`: Teams should play an additional point to decide the winner.

## Half Time

- For 65-minute game half at 7 goals or 30 minutes.(finish the point, if no team has reached 7, add +1 to higher score and take hald when either team reaches this score).

- For 75-minute game half at 8 goals or 40 minutes. (finish the point, if no team has reached 8, add +1 to higher score and take hald when either team reaches this score).

- Half time duration is `2 minutes`.

## Timeouts

- For 65-minute games, each team gets **1 timeout per game**.
- For 75-minute games, each team gets **1 timeout per half**.
- Timeouts cannot be taken within the last 5 minutes of a game.

- Timeout duration is `75 sec`.

## Time Limits and Consequences

- All teams are expected to structly adhere to the timelines between points.
- Offence has 60 seconds to be ready for the subsequent point.
- Defence has 75 seconds to be ready and initiate the pull.
- If Offence is not ready on time, defence calls `delay of game` and shall initiate pull when Offence is ready. Defence is allowed to setup in the same manner as after a time-out has been called.
- If Defence is not ready on time, offence calls `delay of game`, once defence initiates pull, the offence can choose to `brick` the pull regardless of wherever the disc lands.

## Weather & Safety

- **Rain**: Play continues as normal.
- **Heavy rain**: Tournament director's decision is final.
- **Rain and lightning**: Game is suspended immediately. The score at the point of suspension is the final result.
- **Heat**: Please bring caps, sunscreen, and umbrellas. Water will be available on-site.

## Spirit of the Game

Ultimate is a non-contact, self-refereed sport. All players are responsible for administering and adhering to the rules, and for upholding the Spirit of the Game, the principle that fair play is every player's responsibility.

### Key Principles
- Competitive play is encouraged, but must never come at the cost of mutual respect, rules, or the enjoyment of the game.
- All players are expected to know the rules and make fair, honest calls.
- Intentional fouls, cheating, or win-at-all-costs behaviour is contrary to the Spirit of the Game.
