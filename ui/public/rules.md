# Tournament Rules - Sakkath Ultimate Open 2026

## Spirit of the Game

Ultimate is a non-contact, self-refereed sport. All players are responsible for administering and adhering to the rules. Ultimate relies upon a Spirit of the Game that places the responsibility for fair play on every player.

### Key Principles
- Competitive play is encouraged, but should never sacrifice the mutual respect between players, adherence to the agreed-upon rules of the game, or the basic joy of play.
- All players must know the rules and be willing to make a fair and honest call.
- Intentional fouls, cheating, or other win-at-all-costs behavior is contrary to the Spirit of the Game.

## Match Format

### Swiss Rounds
- **Open Division**: 4 Swiss rounds
- **Women Division**: 3 Swiss rounds
- Pairings determined by current standings after each round
- Teams with similar records face each other

### Playoffs & Finals
- Top teams advance based on Swiss round standings
- Playoff format determined by final standings
- Finals determine tournament champion

## Scoring

### Game Points
- Win: 3 points
- Loss: 0 points
- Tiebreakers:
  1. Head-to-head record
  2. Goal differential
  3. Total goals scored
  4. Spirit score average

### Spirit Scores
- Each team rates opponent's spirit on a scale of 0-20
- Spirit score is calculated after each match
- Spirit rankings are separate from game standings

## Field & Equipment

### Field Dimensions
- Standard ultimate field: 100m x 37m
- End zones: 18m deep at each end
- Regulation disc (175g)

### Team Size
- 7 players on field at a time
- Unlimited substitutions (during stoppages)
- Minimum roster: 10 players
- Maximum roster: 18 players

## Time Limits

### Match Duration
- 90 minute time cap
- First to 15 points or highest score at time cap
- Soft cap at 75 minutes (finish current point)
- Hard cap at 90 minutes (stop immediately)

### Timeouts
- 2 timeouts per half
- 1 timeout in overtime
- 75 second duration
- Called by player in possession or team captain

## Fouls & Violations

### Common Infractions
- **Travel**: Establishing pivot foot and moving it before release
- **Pick**: Defensive movement impeded by non-involved player
- **Strip**: Disc knocked from thrower's hand after release motion started
- **Foul**: Non-incidental contact affecting play

### Resolution
- Player making call announces it immediately
- Play stops
- Discuss and resolve the call
- If unresolved, disc returns to thrower
- Contest = replay the point

## Weather & Safety

### Weather Delays
- Lightning: Immediate suspension, 30-minute wait after last strike
- Heavy rain: Captains and tournament director decision
- Extreme heat: Mandatory hydration breaks

### Injury Protocol
- Injured player may be substituted immediately
- Medical timeout does not count against team timeouts
- Player may return to field when cleared

## Code of Conduct

### Expected Behavior
- Respect all players, spectators, and officials
- Maintain control of emotions
- Accept calls gracefully
- Communicate respectfully

### Prohibited Conduct
- Aggressive physical play
- Verbal abuse or intimidation
- Intentional rule violations
- Unsporting behavior

## Tournament Administration

### Check-in
- Team check-in 30 minutes before first match
- Valid ID required for all players
- Roster must be submitted before tournament start

### Protests
- Submit within 15 minutes of match completion
- Must be witnessed by tournament official
- Director decision is final

## Contact Information

For questions or clarifications during the tournament, contact:
- Tournament Director: Available at central tent
- Field Marshals: Present at each field
- Medical Staff: Central first aid station
