# Format

- 6 swiss rounds for both divisions
- Opens: top 8 teams play a Sunday semifinal plus placement final; seeds 9-22 play one Sunday placement game
- Womens: top 4 teams play a Sunday semifinal plus placement final; seeds 5-10 play one Sunday placement game

## Swiss

Both divisions play 6 Swiss rounds.

Your next opponent is determined by your current standings; teams with similar records are paired together, and the system avoids rematches where possible.

### How standings work

Standings are driven by Swiss points:

- Win = 2 points
- Draw = 1 point
- Loss = 0 points

When teams are tied on points, the following tie-breaker criteria are applied in order:

- `c1`: Head-to-head (if all tied teams played against each other)
- `c2`: Median Buchholz (Strength of opponents removing the extreme ends)
- `c3`: Normal Buchholz (Strength of opponents)
- `c4`: Goal difference
- `c5`: Goals scored
- `c6`: Momentum score (Early wins rank higher than later wins)
- `c7`: Random allocation

## Playoffs

### Opens
- Seeds 1-8 are split into two **4-team brackets** for the semifinal round.
  - e.g. 1v4, 2v3 | 5v8, 6v7
- The placement final round then pairs each bracket by standing.
  - e.g. 1v2, 3v4, 5v6, 7v8
- Seeds 9-22 skip the semifinal round and go straight to direct placement games.
  - e.g. 9v10, 11v12, 13v14 ...

### Womens
- Seeds 1-4 play a semifinal round.
  - e.g. 1v4, 2v3
- The placement final round then pairs teams by standing.
  - e.g. 1v2, 3v4
- Seeds 5-10 skip the semifinal round and go straight to direct placement games.
  - e.g. 5v6, 7v8, 9v10

## Game duration

- All Swiss games are 65 mins, with a 15-minute break between scheduled starts.
- Sunday playoff and placement games are 75 mins.
