# Format

- 6 swiss rounds + 2 playoff rounds for Opens
- 6 swiss rounds + 1 final round for Womens

## Swiss

Both divisions play 6 Swiss rounds.

Your next opponent is determined by your current standings; teams with similar records are paired together, and the system avoids rematches where possible.

### How standings work

Standings are driven by Swiss points:

- Win = 2 points
- Draw = 1 point
- Loss = 0 points

When teams are tied on points, the following tie-breaker criteria are applied in order:

- `c1`: Head-to-head result between the two tied teams
- `c2`: Median Buchholz (Strength of opponents faced)
- `c3`: Goal difference
- `c4`: Goals scored
- `c5`: Momentum score (e.g. WWLL ranks higher than LLWW)
- `c6`: Random allocation

## Playoffs

### Opens
- After Swiss, teams are split into **brackets of 4** for the semifinal round.
  - e.g. 1v4, 2v3 | 5v8, 6v7 ... (with seed swap within each bracket)
- The final round then pairs each team against their neighbour by standing.
  - e.g. 1v2, 3v4, 5v6 ...

### Womens
- No semifinal round, a direct final round is played, pairing teams by standing.
  - e.g. 1v2, 3v4, 5v6 ...

## Game duration

- All women division games are 75 mins each.
- Swiss games for open division are 60 mins each, while playoff games are 75 mins.
